Welcome to Bardhe Clinic

Bardhe Clinic Multispecialist Hospital is dedicated to providing compassionate, reliable, and personalized healthcare for every patient and family in the community. Under the care of Dr. Lokesh Ujjwal Bardhe, the clinic offers general health checkups, fever and infection treatment, minor surgical procedures, wound dressing and suturing, preventive health consultation, and practical medical guidance with a patient-first approach. With easy appointment booking, supportive communication, and a convenient location near New Bus Stand, Jarud, Ta: Warud, Dist: Amravati, Bardhe Clinic aims to make quality healthcare accessible, comfortable, and trustworthy for everyone.
